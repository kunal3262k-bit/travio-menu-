# BACKLOG

This is the parking lot for ideas and "nice-to-have" features. 
As the Founding Staff Engineer, I will NOT build these until the core North Star Journey is completely optimized and restaurants are happily paying for the product.

## Future Modules
- **Inventory Management**: Tracking ingredient stock levels. *Deferred*: Restaurants can survive without it on day one; it adds too much onboarding friction.
- **Payroll & HR**: Managing staff shifts and salaries. *Deferred*: Completely disconnected from the customer ordering journey.
- **CRM & Loyalty Programs**: Points systems and customer retention. *Deferred*: We need to get them the first order before worrying about the 10th order.
- **Coupons & Discounts**: Promo codes. *Deferred*: Adds complexity to the billing flow.
- **Reservations**: Booking tables in advance. *Deferred*: A different business model entirely.
- **Delivery Integration**: Zomato/Swiggy or direct delivery. *Deferred*: Focus is 100% on dine-in QR ordering first.
- **Advanced Analytics Dashboard**: Charts and graphs. *Deferred*: Restaurant owners care about cash in the register today, not charts.
- **Marketing Automation & AI Chatbot**: *Deferred*: Over-engineering.
- **POS / Hardware Integrations**: *Deferred*: Requires massive sales/support overhead.
- **Multi-Branch / Franchise**: *Deferred*: Targeting small/medium single-location restaurants first.
