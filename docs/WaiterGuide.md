# DineFlow Waiter Guide

This guide is for the floor staff operating the Waiter Dashboard.

## 1. The Waiter Dashboard
Keep your phone or tablet open to the Waiter Dashboard (`/waiter`).
You will receive real-time notifications for three things:
1. **Food Ready**: The kitchen has finished preparing an order. Pick it up and deliver it to the specified table.
2. **Customer Calls**: A customer needs assistance at their table (e.g., they need water or have a question).
3. **Bill Requests**: A customer has requested the bill. 

## 2. Managing Requests
When you see a request on your dashboard:
1. Walk to the table and assist the customer.
2. Click the **Resolve** or **Complete** button on the request card to clear it from your screen.

## 3. Taking Cash Payments
If a customer selects "Pay by Cash":
1. Bring the cash register bill to the table.
2. Collect the cash.
3. On your Waiter Dashboard, find their order and click **Mark as Paid**. This is critical for the restaurant's daily closing report.
