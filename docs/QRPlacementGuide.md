# SwiftTab QR Placement Guide

Setting up your QR codes correctly is the most important step for a successful SwiftTab launch.

## 1. Generating QRs
1. Go to **Setup > Tables** in your Admin Dashboard.
2. Add all your tables (e.g., Table 1, Table 2, Balcony 1).
3. Click **Print All QRs**. This will generate a PDF file.

## 2. Printing
- **Material**: We recommend printing on glossy sticker paper or thick cardstock. If printing on normal paper, place the QR in an acrylic stand.
- **Size**: The QR code should be at least 2x2 inches (5x5 cm) to ensure older phones can scan it easily.

## 3. Placement Strategy
- **Visibility**: Place the QR code on the corner of the table, not the center where plates will cover it.
- **Lighting**: Ensure the QR code is in a well-lit area. Avoid placing it directly under harsh spotlights which cause glare on acrylic stands.
- **Call to Action**: Make sure the text "Scan to Order" is clearly visible next to the QR code so customers know what to do.
