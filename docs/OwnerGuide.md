# DineFlow Restaurant Owner Guide

Welcome to DineFlow! This guide covers everything you need to know to manage your restaurant using the Admin Dashboard.

## 1. Initial Setup
1. Log in to your Admin Dashboard.
2. Go to **Settings > Profile** and fill in your restaurant name, address, and phone number.
3. Go to **Settings > Branding** to upload your Logo and Cover Image. Choose a brand color that matches your restaurant.

## 2. Managing Your Menu
1. Go to **Setup > Menu** to create Categories (e.g., Starters, Main Course).
2. Inside each category, add your Items with their price and veg/non-veg status.
3. Use the **AI Importer** if you have photos of your physical menu.

## 3. Daily Operations
- **Kitchen Dashboard**: Give your kitchen staff the `/kitchen` link. They will see orders appear instantly.
- **Waiter Dashboard**: Give your waiters the `/waiter` link. They will see requests for water, bill, and assistance.
- **Close Day**: At the end of the day, go to the Home tab and click **Close Day** to print your daily sales summary.

## 4. Temporary Closures
If the kitchen gets too busy, go to **Settings > Operations** and change the Restaurant Status to **Temporarily Busy**. Customers will see a wait time message but won't be able to place orders until you switch it back to **Live**.
