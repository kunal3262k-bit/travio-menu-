SwiftTab Sales Playbook
=========================

Restaurant owners are incredibly busy, often stressed, and pitched to constantly. The golden rule of restaurant sales is: Be brief, focus on their pain (time/money), and show, don't just tell.

Here are the exact scripts you should use when contacting your list of 493 leads.

--------------------------------------------------

📞 Strategy 1: The Cold Call (The "Send the Video" Approach)
*Do not try to explain the whole software over the phone. The only goal of the cold call is to get permission to send them your 50-second cinematic demo video.*

You: "Hi, is the owner or manager around?"

(If Yes / When they get on the line)

You: "Hi, I know you're super busy so I'll be quick. I'm a local software developer here in Delhi. I noticed you guys get really busy during peak hours, so I built a system that lets your customers order directly from their phones by scanning a QR code on the table."

You: "It cuts waiter turnaround times in half and pushes orders straight to your kitchen. I have a quick 50-second video showing exactly how it works. Can I WhatsApp the video to you real quick just to see if you like the concept?"

* Why this works: You aren't asking for a meeting or their money. You are just asking to send a 50-second video on WhatsApp. It's very low friction.

-----------------------------

(When they get on the line)

You: "Hi, mujhe pata hai aap busy honge toh main seedhe point par aata hoon. Main Delhi se ek software developer hoon. Maine dekha hai ki weekends par yahan kaafi bheed hoti hai, toh maine ek system banaya hai jisse customers table par rakhe QR code ko scan karke seedhe apne phone se order kar sakte hain."

--------------------------------------------------

🚶 Strategy 2: The Walk-In (The "Physical Demo" Approach)
*The most effective way to sell restaurant software is in person, during their "dead hours" (usually between 3:30 PM and 5:00 PM).*

Preparation: 
1. Print a physical QR code on a nice piece of cardstock. 
2. Have your phone (acting as the customer) and a tablet (acting as the kitchen) ready.

The Pitch:
"Hi, are you the owner? I'm [Your Name], a local developer. I've built something that is going to save you a lot of money on staffing and increase your table turnover during busy nights. Do you have 60 seconds? I just want to show you."

(Place the QR code on the counter)

"Scan this with your phone. Notice how there's no app to download? Your customer just sees your menu immediately. Go ahead, tap the Wagyu Burger and hit order."

(Hold up the Kitchen Tablet)

"Look here—the exact second you hit order, it pops up on the kitchen tablet. No waiters running back and forth, no miswritten tickets. Your waiters can focus on hospitality, while the software handles the ordering."

The Close: "I'm looking for 3 flagship restaurants in this area to launch this with for a massive discount. I'll set up your entire menu for free using our AI. Can we pilot this on just 3 of your tables next week?"

🛡️ Handling Objections
-------------------------

Objection 1: "We already have enough waiters." / "Humare paas kaafi waiters hain."
Your Response: "That's great! SwiftTab isn't meant to replace your best staff—it's meant to take the robotic order-taking off their plate so they can focus on hospitality, upselling drinks, and making sure the customers are actually happy."
Hindi: "Bahut badhiya sir! SwiftTab ka kaam aapke staff ko nikalna nahi hai. Iska kaam hai unko order likhne ke stress se bachana, taaki woh customers ki service par zyada dhyan de sakein aur drinks ya desserts upsell kar sakein."

Objection 2: "My customers aren't tech-savvy, they like physical menus." / "Humare customers ko physical menu hi pasand hai."
Your Response: "I completely understand. You don't have to remove physical menus. Think of SwiftTab as an addition. Give physical menus to those who want them, but leave the QR code on the table. Most people under 40 will default to the QR code."
Hindi: "Main samajh raha hoon. Aapko apne printed menus hatane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Jo physical menu maangega usko wahi dijiye, par table par QR code bhi rakhne dijiye. Aap hairan honge ki aaj-kal young log directly phone se order karna zyada pasand karte hain."

Objection 3: "We use Zomato/Swiggy for POS, we don't need another system." / "Hum already Zomato/Swiggy use karte hain."
Your Response: "Those are great for delivery, but they charge massive commissions and they don't help your dine-in efficiency. SwiftTab is strictly to make your dine-in experience faster."
Hindi: "Sir, Zomato/Swiggy delivery ke liye bahut aache hain, par unki commission bahut zyada hoti hai. SwiftTab strictly aapke 'dine-in' customers (jo yahan baith kar khate hain) ke liye hai, taaki service tez ho aur aap weekends par zyada log attend kar sakein."
